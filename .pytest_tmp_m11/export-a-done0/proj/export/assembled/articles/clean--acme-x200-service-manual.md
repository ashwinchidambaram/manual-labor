# Acme X200 Service Manual

## Acme X200 Service Manual

Model X200 · Revision 4

## 1. Safety and Preparation

* Torque wrench, 10 to 60 N·m
* Feeler gauge set
* Socket set with M8 socket
* Clean rags

* Disconnect the 12 V supply
* Drain the coolant reservoir
* Remove the cover plate
* Tag all connectors

## 4. Cooling and Lubrication

* Fit the cover plate
* Torque the bolts in sequence
* Reconnect the 12 V supply
* Clear the service log

* Torque wrench, 10 to 60 N·m
* Feeler gauge set
* Socket set with M8 socket
* Clean rags
