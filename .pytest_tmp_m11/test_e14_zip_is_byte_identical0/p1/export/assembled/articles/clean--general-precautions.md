# General Precautions

### 1.1 General Precautions

Use only M8 fasteners when refitting the gearbox housing. Do not operate the unit while the pressure valve is removed. Tighten the cooling fan bolts to 35 N·m in a cross pattern.

Use only M8 fasteners when refitting the pressure valve. Wear protective gloves when handling the mounting bracket. Compare the wiring harness against the reference illustration before refitting. The lubrication procedure for the fuel filter is described in the paragraphs below.

Rotate the drive belt through 90° until it seats fully. Do not operate the unit while the mounting bracket is removed. Compare the drive belt against the reference illustration before refitting.

Rotate the mounting bracket through 90° until it seats fully. Clean the fuel filter with a lint-free cloth and allow it to dry completely. Allow the unit to cool before touching the mounting bracket. The arrangement of the drive belt is shown in the illustration (see Figure 1). The arrangement of the pressure valve is shown in the illustration (see Figure 2). The arrangement of the bearing carrier is shown in the illustration (see Figure 3).
