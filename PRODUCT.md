# Product
## Register
product
## Users
Technicians and documentation owners turning equipment manuals (PDF, scanned PDF, DOCX) into a knowledge base an AI agent will answer from. Often first-time users of the tool, reviewing for an hour at a desk, keyboard-first once they learn it. They are accountable if a torque value is wrong.
## Product Purpose
Get a faithful, reviewed knowledge base out of messy manuals with the least human review, and show the reviewer why each decision is safe.
## Brand Personality
Friendly, calm and approachable, like a knowledgeable colleague walking you through it, and still exact and accountable. Warm without filler: no chatty padding, no confetti, no hype. Progress is acknowledged quietly and honestly.
## Anti-references
Consumer onboarding carousels and confetti; AI products that show a single "confidence" number with no reason; dark "hacker" dashboards; marketing gradients.
## Design Principles
1. Show the evidence, not a verdict. 2. Say what will happen before it happens. 3. Never hide a count: totals reconcile on screen. 4. Explanations live where the decision is. 5. Keyboard first, mouse equal. 6. Guide, don't lecture: one clear next step at a time, in plain words a first-time user understands. 7. Build trust by showing, not telling: the source page, the diff, the checks, the progress made. 8. Jargon is translated at the point of use (a human title before an identifier, a sentence before a score).
## Accessibility & Inclusion
WCAG 2.2 AA; visible focus everywhere; never colour only; works at 200% zoom; screen-reader names for every control.
